Why COLLABO

Ideas here can't just be talk.

Everyone builds a pitch feed. COLLABO is built around what happens after the pitch: who came, who was picked, and whether anything shipped.

The accountability layer

Space not created

Post an idea, never vet anyone and never form a team, and the post carries a public tag saying so. Walking away from an idea is visible.

Removal records

Removing someone takes a stated reason, and it is recorded. The person removed can appeal.

Credentials

Every profile lists what its owner has actually done, including the spaces they formed. Reputation is earned by what exists.

Why post publicly, not message five friends?

Reach

Five talented friends are five people. A public post puts your idea in front of an unlimited horizon of them.

Inspiration

An idea feeds other ideas. Someone reading your call for graphic designers, or your plan for a film crew in your city, gets sparked into their own work. The Gaze is a home for inspiration, not only a recruitment board.

Five friends are proven. A stranger isn't. That is exactly why credentials and shipped links matter: they make strangers viable.

People decide, not an algorithm

Human vetting

The poster, and any co-founders they bring in, read every application and choose. The platform never matches or assigns anyone.

Dignity built in

Whether anyone applied at all stays between you and the app. A post nobody answered simply reads as closed.

Quiet teammates handled fairly

A response clock, a chance for someone to speak up for a quiet teammate, and a visible cancel button for the founder. Nobody is purged on a whim.

There's no hiding from greatness.
