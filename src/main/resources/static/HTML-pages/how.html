How it works

From idea to team, in five steps.

Your people are out there. Here is how you find them.

  1. Post your idea to The Gaze

    The Gaze is the public feed where people scroll for ideas to join. Set how long you will take applications, or leave it open. Want a partner first? Invite a collaborator and vet together.

  2. People apply with a why-you

    No bare Apply button. Each applicant writes a few sentences on why they fit, and links their credentials.

  3. You review, by hand

    Work through the cards and Accept, Decline, or Shortlist to set someone aside for now. Co-founders keep their own shortlists, so agreement and disagreement are visible.

  4. Accepted people choose to join

    They can read everything about the idea first. The chats open only when they press Join space, because joining is a choice.

  5. Build in your Yarnspaces

    Yarn is our word for message. MySpace holds private yarns, WeSpace is where co-founders decide, and your Workspace is where the team builds the MVP. Milestones and payments are logged right there.

Your people are out there. Let's collabo.
